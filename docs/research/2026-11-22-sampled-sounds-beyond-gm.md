# Which sampled sounds to add beyond General MIDI, and in what order

- **Date**: 2026-11-22
- **Asked by**: #349 (the owner, 2026-11-20: "expanding the sounds Reprise can play that are not MIDI, with better guitars and the missing instruments")

## Question

GeneralUser GS plays every track, and three curated SFZ packs (Swagbass, Growlybass, Emilyguitar) install from the Sounds screen. Emilyguitar's "Five voices" is the default of both guitar families (#346), so an acoustic track plays an electric guitar. Mandolin plays GeneralUser's Mandolin variation, banjo GM program 105, and a 12-string is any guitar sound doubled by the engine (#416, #424). Which free packs would make the guitars better and fill the missing families (acoustic steel and nylon, bass finger and pick, drums, piano, strings, banjo, mandolin, 12-string)? What does the sampler lack to play them well? Which should be bundled and which downloaded, against the installer size and the 300 MiB HQ memory budget? The answer is a ranked shortlist, one follow-up issue per item.

## What was tried

- **Sources.** The sfzinstruments catalogue (guitars, pianos, drums, strings, folk) and every repository of the `sfzinstruments` GitHub organisation (licences from the GitHub API and the `LICENSE` files); FreePats (`freepats.zenvoid.org`); Versilian (VSCO 2 CE, VCSL); the AVL Drumkits page; the Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra repository; Virtual Playing Orchestra; the Unreal Instruments site; the Blue Jeans and Moonbeams document; web searches for free mandolin, banjo and 12-string samples. Every licence below was read on its source page or in its repository's licence file on **2026-11-22**, the key line is quoted, and the page is named. A commercial library is out of scope, and so is anything sold, even cheaply.
- **Downloads.** 24 archives (3.3 GB) went into a scratch directory outside the repository: Black and Green Guitars, Shinyguitar, Emilyguitar, Meatbass, the Karoryfer x bigcat cello, Big Rusty Drums, Osiris Piano, ganjo, the Kay 5-String Banjo, Terkelsen's Mandolin, the Ovation Guitar, Damien's Funky Guitar, AVL Red Zeppelin, and from FreePats the Spanish Classical Guitar, the FSS Steel-String Guitar, the FSBS electric guitars (Clean #1, the small Clean #1, Jazz, Direct, Distorted #2), the YR finger and pick basses, Upright Piano KW and MuldjordKit. Nothing was committed and nothing in them was run. The 7z and tar.xz archives were unpacked with `bsdtar`.
- **Loading.** `pnpm sfz:check <dir>` on each unpacked pack. It walks every `.sfz` under the directory, so the include fragments a pack keeps in a `modules/` or `mappings/` folder report "no regions" or missing samples on their own. Only the programs at the top level count, and the tables say "loads" when every top-level program passed.
- **Memory.** Two small Python scripts in the scratch directory read the WAV `data` chunk and the FLAC STREAMINFO of every sample (channels × frames × 4 bytes, which is what the memory manager holds once decoded, per the audio README), and add up every sample a program's regions map over the instrument's range: guitar MIDI 40 to 88 (24 frets in standard tuning, as in the HQ budget test), bass 28 to 67, piano 21 to 108, drums 35 to 81, mandolin 55 to 88, banjo 48 to 88, cello 36 to 76. That is an **upper bound per instrument**: every velocity layer, round robin, random take and release sample over the whole range, which is what the planner preloads when a song reaches all of them (it asks `RegionMap.candidates()` for every take). The parse follows `#include`, `#define`, `default_path` and the header inheritance, and like our loader it ignores CC conditions, so a pack whose layers are chosen by a controller counts every layer. On Emilyguitar it gives 144.8 MiB for `emily_five_voice.sfz` (204 files), close to the 216-file Five-voices shape the HQ budget test models.
- **The engine.** `packages/audio/src/sfz/opcodes.ts` (what is played, counted or refused), `preprocess.ts` (the include limits), the audio README (Sampler, SFZ loader, Articulations, Effects chain, Memory manager), `apps/web/src/features/sounds/` (the curated list, the installer, default sounds), ADR-0003, ADR-0005, ADR-0028 and the budgets table in `docs/plans/roadmap.md`.

## Findings

### The budgets the candidates are measured against

- **Memory.** "Decoded audio resident, HQ tier, default" is 300 MB (the 300 MiB `HQ_BUDGET_BYTES`). The budget counts distinct instruments, not tracks: tracks that play the same pack instrument share one resident copy, as the budget row's third case shows (#346: four guitar tracks on one Five-voices pack hold 223.5 MiB in all). The worst measured case is four *distinct* synthetic Swagbass-shaped packs at 70.9 MiB each, 283.6 MiB. So the figure to judge is the sum of the upper bounds of the distinct instruments one song uses. Past 300 MiB the manager evicts, and a note whose sample is not resident is skipped; an instrument whose own bound is over 300 MiB cannot be resident at all.
- **Installer.** The budgets table has no installer row. ADR-0003 accepts "an installer around 150 MB". On the last `Release` run on `main` (44953020289, 2026-11-21) the Windows artifact was 150.2 MB, and each macOS artifact (a dmg and a zip together) about 300 MB. GeneralUser GS is 32.3 MB of each installer. So every megabyte bundled is a megabyte past the figure ADR-0003 accepted.

### What the sampler already plays

The SFZ loader and the sampler already handle what most free guitar packs are built from: velocity layers, round robins by `seq_length`/`seq_position` and by `lorand`/`hirand` (the sink rotates them, the planner preloads them all), release triggers with `rt_decay`, `trigger=first` and `trigger=legato` (a hammer-on or pull-off plays the legato regions), one-way choke groups (`group`/`off_by`), and keyswitch layers. A `sw_label` that names an articulation becomes a set at load (`Hammer-on` is `legato`, `Staccato` is `staccato`, `Palm Mute` is `palmMute`). Amp and cabinet simulation exists too: WP-092's chain gives every electric track a drive, tone and generated-cabinet stage (`clean`, `overdrive`, `distortion`, `fuzz`). So the issue's examples of what might be missing (round robins, release triggers, key switches, an amp) are not the gap. The gaps are below.

### What the sampler lacks for the best candidates

In order of how many good candidates each one blocks:

1. **CC-conditioned regions.** `locc`/`hicc` and `set_cc` are counted as unsupported and the region is kept, so every variant a controller should choose sounds at once. Black and Green (the tailpiece copies), Black and Blue (`hicc105`), Shinyguitar (microphone blend and voice count), the cello (voice count), Big Rusty Drums (hat openness, microphones) and Osiris (three microphones) all choose layers this way. The fix is in the loader: evaluate each condition against the `set_cc` default (0 when there is none) and drop the regions that cannot play, counting them in the report. No engine or UI change is needed. Size M.
2. **Long, stereo, many-take samples against the budget.** The FSBS banks hold four random takes of 13 to 16 s stereo at 48 kHz per zone, about 7 MiB a file, 372 to 761 MiB on one track. The planner preloads every take (`candidates()`), which is right for small packs and fatal here. Either the planner keeps one take resident under pressure and rotates the others in, or such packs are trimmed and mirrored (the ADR below). Until one of these happens, a full FSBS bank cannot be curated.
3. **Archives other than zip.** FreePats ships 7z and tar.xz, BJAM ships RAR, and our reader (ADR-0028) takes zip only. A user can unpack and drop the folder today, so these packs can be listed as drop-only, but they cannot be direct downloads or the default offer of a family. Writing an LZMA reader is a dependency or a large module; mirroring the open-licence ones as zip is not (the ADR below).
4. **Non-GM drum maps.** MuldjordKit (and most multi-microphone kits) put their pieces on keys of their own, and even AVL Red Zeppelin puts a stick click on GM's second kick (35) and a hat swish and a crash choke on GM's two high toms (48, 50). A curated pack can only point at the pack's own files, so there is no way to ship a remapping SFZ of ours. The same mirror would carry one, or the curated list could carry SFZ text that the installer writes beside the pack's files.
5. **Smaller gaps, each tolerable:** `amp_velcurve_N` (every Karoryfer pack: the velocity response differs from the author's), filters and filter envelopes (Upright Piano KW's soft layer, Shinyguitar's tone), LFO vibrato and `bend_*` (our automation plays vibrato and bends anyway), `note_polyphony`, `note_selfmask` and `off_mode` (a repeated piano or banjo note stacks voices), `width`, `$sample_dir` from a Sforzando bank (Shinyguitar), the 256 include-read limit (Big Rusty's full kit, the banjo's chord split), and AIFF samples (the Discord SFZ GM bank, a work in progress that is not a candidate yet).

Amp and cabinet simulation is **not** a gap that changes direction. The DI-recorded candidates (Emilyguitar, Black and Green, FSBS Direct) are what WP-092's chain was built for: `overdrive` and `distortion` give crunch and high-gain on top of them, within the known aliasing limit at high notes. A pre-distorted bank (FSBS Distorted #2) sounds more like a real amp but costs 664 MiB a track and cannot palm-mute, so it is no better a route than the chain. No ADR is proposed for an amp simulator in the audio package.

Palm mutes, harmonics, slides and dead notes are sampled by none of the candidates with a clear licence (BJAM has palm mutes and harmonics but no download link). For every pack in the shortlist they stay synthesized on the default layer, as ADR-0004 and WP-093 do today, and only legato and staccato get real layers (Black and Green).

### Bundled or downloaded

- **Bundle nothing new now.** The installer is already at ADR-0003's figure, and the only candidates small enough to bundle (the FreePats nylon guitar at 4.7 MB, the YR basses at 3 MB each, the small FSBS clean guitar at 3 MB, the small Upright Piano KW at 3 MB) are single-layer banks that nobody has compared by ear with GeneralUser GS. If a listening test finds a starter set that beats GM on acoustic guitar, bass or piano, about 15 MB of FLAC could be bundled, and the owner would first add an installer-size row to the budgets table (for example 175 MB on Windows) so that the cost is decided, not accumulated.
- **Download the shortlist**, from the Sounds screen. Measured alone, most shortlisted instruments are small: AVL Red Zeppelin 54.6 MiB, the nylon 18.9 MiB, the steel 50.3 MiB, Upright Piano KW 109.4 MiB, the banjos 21 to 32 MiB, the YR basses under 10 MiB. Three are not covered by that: Pastabass and VSCO are not measured, the bowed cello is 108.9 MiB, and Black and Green is over the budget in any band song (rank 10).
- **A band song is what decides it.** Drums, electric, bass and acoustic, each on its upper bound: AVL Red Zeppelin 54.6 + Emilyguitar Five voices 144.8 + Swagbass clean 149.4 + FSS steel 50.3 = **399.1 MiB**, a third over the budget. Today's defaults alone (Emilyguitar on both guitar families, Swagbass chosen for bass) are 294.2 MiB of it. A real song reaches fewer keys and velocities than the bound, so its resident figure is lower, but nobody has measured one. So downloading the shortlist is safe one pack at a time, while a song that uses several of them may evict and skip notes. Before the picks are combined as defaults, one issue should measure a typical band song with them and, if it is over, make the planner keep one round robin or take per key resident under pressure (the second gap above); Emilyguitar alone holds three takes of every note.
- **Direct download or drop-only.** Under ADR-0028 a direct download needs an open licence, a stable archive URL, and a hash and size from one real download. AVL (CC BY-SA), ganjo, Black and Green, the cello and VSCO (CC0) qualify as zips at their sources. The FreePats packs are CC0 (and the steel guitar GPL) but 7z or tar.xz, so they are drop-only until they are mirrored as zip. The Kay banjo has no release archive, only a repository tarball, so it is drop-only too. ADR-0028 names "CC0, CC BY, an open source licence" and not CC BY-SA. BY-SA is an open licence, and a direct download from the author's own server is not redistribution by Reprise, so AVL qualifies; the rank-1 issue should record that reading in sound-packs.md. AVL's URL is the weak point: the zip sits in a plain directory listing and has already been replaced once (the file is `RED_ZEPPELIN_2023_repack.zip`). The hash keeps a changed file out, but the next repack breaks the download until the list is re-measured.

### A direction change, and the ADR it needs

The best small packs for the missing families (nylon, steel, piano, basses) are FreePats 7z archives, the memory-heavy FSBS banks would need trimming, and a non-GM drum kit would need a remapping SFZ. All three need the same thing ADR-0028 does not allow today: **a curated pack fetched from a Reprise-owned mirror** (a zip on a release of this repository) **instead of its original source**, possibly re-encoded or trimmed, possibly with SFZ files of our own, for packs whose licence allows it, on the terms below. Proposed: an ADR amending ADR-0028, which decides whether mirrors are allowed, which licences qualify, how the mirror is versioned and hashed, how attribution and share-alike are shown on the Sounds screen (it shows the licence name and link today, not the author), and whether a trimmed pack keeps the upstream id. This note does not write it. Without it the shortlist still works, with FreePats packs as drop-only.

What each licence asks of a mirror:

- **CC0**: nothing; credit is a courtesy. **CC BY** (MuldjordKit, Salamander): credit the author and link the licence.
- **CC BY-SA** (AVL): a trimmed copy or a remap SFZ is an adaptation. It is released under CC BY-SA, credits the author (AVL Drumkits, Glen MacArthur), links the licence and says what was changed. The AVL page asks for attribution exactly when the samples or the library files are modified, which is the remap case.
- **GPL** (FSS steel, the Kay banjo): the mirror carries the licence text and an offer of, or a link to, the preferred form for making modifications, which a trimmed or re-encoded file is not. For FSS that is the FreePats WAV and SFZ release. For the Kay banjo, `LICENSE.txt` says "The source code being the .gig file (of which contains the audio samples and settings)", and the sfzinstruments repository carries no `.gig` (its samples were also edited "to reduce noise"), so the link has to reach FlameStudios' original. The ADR may instead keep GPL packs out of the mirror.
- **Reprise's own code**: fetching a GPL or BY-SA pack from its source, or mirroring it as a separate release asset, does not change the licence of Reprise's code. The packs are separate data files the app reads at run time, an aggregation and not a combined work, and only the mirrored pack itself carries share-alike and source obligations. The repository has no `LICENSE` file today, so the ADR should name the code licence it assumes; choosing that licence is the owner's call, and this note does not add one.

## Decisions for the owner, in order

1. **The mirror ADR** (amending ADR-0028, above). It decides whether ranks 2 to 6 below are a download and a family default or only a link: without it the FreePats 7z and tar.xz packs are drop-only (the user finds the page, unpacks the archive and drops the folder), FSS cannot replace Emilyguitar as the acoustic default, and AVL's wrong keys cannot be remapped.
2. **CC-conditioned regions** in the SFZ loader (size M). It unblocks the cello, the Karoryfer basses and keyswitch libraries.
3. **Memory in a band song**: accept eviction when a song combines several packs (399.1 MiB at the upper bound for drums, electric, bass and acoustic), or first measure a real song and make the planner keep one take per key resident under pressure.
4. **Then the picks** from the shortlist.

## Conclusion

Open until the owner picks: no ADR has been accepted and no follow-up issue filed yet. The recommendation, in order: decide the proposed ADR on mirroring curated packs (amending ADR-0028), since it decides whether most of the shortlist is a download or a link; implement CC-conditioned regions before any Karoryfer keyswitch library; measure a band song against the 300 MiB budget before combining the picks as defaults; bundle nothing new; then add the shortlist in rank order. The licence of the Unreal Instruments packs, one of the planning questions in the research index, stays unresolved: their pages carry no terms.
